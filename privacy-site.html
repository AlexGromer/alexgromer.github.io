Privacy of this site

Last updated 30 October 2026

This site collects nothing. This page is about the website only. The policy for the rclone-colab application is a separate document: rclone-colab privacy policy.

What the site does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • No cookies.
  • No forms, accounts or comments.
  • No third-party requests: fonts, scripts, styles and images are served from this site, and a content security policy forbids anything else.

What is stored on your device

If you use the theme button, the site stores one value, theme (light or dark), in your browser's local storage so the choice survives a reload. It never leaves your device and I cannot read it. Clear your site data to remove it.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub processes technical request data such as your IP address and browser details under its own Privacy Statement. I do not receive those logs and have no access to them.

Links to other sites

Links lead to GitHub and a few other sites. Once you follow one, that site's policy applies.

Changes and contact

Changes are published here with a new date. Questions: through GitHub.

Code of conduct

Last updated 30 October 2026

I want my repositories to be places where people can report a bug, ask a question or send a patch without having a bad time. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, comments and any other space tied to a repository under AlexGromer.

Expected

  • Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
  • Be concrete and patient: give a way to reproduce, explain your reasoning, accept that maintainers have limited time.
  • Accept a "no" on scope or direction gracefully. Forking is always an option.

Not acceptable

  • Harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory language or imagery.
  • Publishing other people's private information.
  • Sustained disruption of discussions, spam or advertising.
  • Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

Enforcement

As the maintainer I may edit, hide or delete comments, lock threads, close pull requests, and block people from my repositories. Typical steps are a warning, a temporary block, and a permanent block for serious or repeated cases. I decide, and I will explain the decision when it is safe to do so.

Reporting

  • Use GitHub's own tools: Report content on the comment, or the report abuse form for a user. I act on what I see and on what GitHub passes on.
  • If you are not sure whether something crosses the line, say so in the thread without naming anyone, and I will look.
  • Reports are handled in confidence as far as the platform allows. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.

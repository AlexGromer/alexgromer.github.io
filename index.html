rclone-colab

rclone-colab is a personal, single-user application: an OAuth client that lets my own tooling (rclone running inside a Google Colab runtime or on my workstation) read and write files in my own Google Drive. It is used to keep model evaluation runs, checkpoints and logs in one Drive folder without granting access interactively on every runtime start.

  • Purpose: synchronise one Google Drive folder of the account owner with compute sessions the owner starts.
  • Users: the account owner only. The application is not offered to third parties.
  • Data: files in the owner's Google Drive, accessed under the drive scope, on the owner's instruction.
  • Policies: privacy policy, terms of service.

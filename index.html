Principles

Three rules I keep coming back to

  • Fail closed

    When a guard cannot decide, it denies. Defaults deny, exceptions are explicit and logged, and a broken check never turns into a pass.

  • Reproducible

    Pinned sources, signed commits, deterministic output. If I cannot rebuild it from what is written down, I do not trust it.

  • Worse is better

    A small, simple thing that works today beats a complete one that ships never. Ship the part that holds, then iterate.

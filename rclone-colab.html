What rclone-colab does

rclone-colab is an OAuth client for Google Drive used by one person: the developer. It lets the developer's own copy of rclone, running inside a Google Colab runtime or on the developer's workstation, read and write files in the developer's own Google Drive folder. Its only job is to keep model evaluation runs, checkpoints and logs in that folder without granting Drive access by hand every time a compute session starts.

Who can use it

Only the developer's Google account. The application is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else.

Data it accesses

  • Files in the authorising account's Google Drive, under the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope, on the developer's instruction and within one folder the developer chooses.
  • OAuth tokens issued by Google, stored only in the developer's own password manager and Colab Secrets.

No data is shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models. Details: privacy policy · terms of service.

Revoking access

At any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

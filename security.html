Security policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

I take reports seriously and handle them as a person with a day job would: promptly when I can, openly about the limits. This policy covers every repository owned by AlexGromer that is not a fork, and this website. For a fork, report to the upstream project.

Report privately

  • Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: open the Security tab of the affected repository and choose Report a vulnerability. For this site use this form.
  • Please do not open a public issue or pull request for a vulnerability.
  • Include the repository, version or commit, steps to reproduce, and the impact you see. Do not include other people's secrets or personal data.

A machine-readable copy of this is in /.well-known/security.txt.

What to expect

These are targets for personal projects, not a service-level agreement.

  • Acknowledgement within 7 days.
  • First assessment within 14 days: confirmed, not reproducible, or out of scope.
  • A fix or a mitigation plan within 90 days. Coordinated disclosure: I publish an advisory once a fix is out, or after 90 days, whichever comes first, and I will agree a date with you if more time is needed.
  • Credit in the advisory if you want it.

Supported versions

The latest release, or the default branch where there are no releases. See support and maintenance.

No bounty

I do not run a bounty programme and cannot pay for reports. I can say thanks in public.

Scope

  • In scope: the code, scripts, workflows and published releases in my non-fork repositories, and this website.
  • Out of scope: my home network and servers (do not probe them), social engineering, denial of service, findings in third-party platforms such as GitHub itself, and reports about a dependency without a demonstrated effect on the project.

Safe harbour

If you act in good faith, stay inside the scope above, avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption, and give me reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it, I will not take legal action against you or report you for the research. I can only speak for myself, not for GitHub or other third parties.
